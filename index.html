The problem

Checking rooftop panels by hand is slow and risky

Dust, bird droppings, snow and damage reduce the power a panel produces. Solar is growing fast in Pakistan, so owners need a quick and safe way to know when a panel needs attention.

Our solution

Take a photo, upload it, and get the panel’s condition with a confidence score and a suggested action, in a couple of seconds, from your phone or laptop.

How it works

Three simple steps

No special equipment. Just a photo of the panel.

1

Upload a photo

Take a picture of the solar panel or choose one from your gallery.

2

AI analyses it

Three deep learning models study the image and vote on the condition.

3

Get the result

See the condition, how confident the AI is, and what action to take.

What it detects

Six panel conditions

Each condition comes with a simple suggested action.

Clean

No action needed.

Dusty

Rinse with water and wipe with a soft cloth.

Snow-Covered

Remove snow gently; do not scrape the glass.

Bird-drop

Wipe off the droppings soon, then rinse.

Electrical Damage

Switch off the system and call a technician.

Physical Damage

Get the panel inspected; it may need replacing.

Technology

Built with computer vision, machine learning and deep learning

We trained on 869 solar panel images from Kaggle (6 classes) and compared classic ML with modern deep learning.

Machine learning

Shape features (HOG) and color features (HSV histogram) feed Random Forest, SVM and KNN models.

Deep learning

Transfer learning with MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0 and ResNet50, combined as an ensemble for the final answer.

Computer vision

Images are cleaned, resized to 224 x 224 and split into training, validation and test sets.
